            Built on Three Pillars of Agentic Security.

            AgentDNA verifies identity and delegated authority, enforces context-aware access throughout agentic workflows and preserves tamper-evident proof of every protected interaction.

            GRADE

            Evolved Identity

            Identity goes beyond an identifier, encompassing ownership, inherited roles, delegated authority, historical actions and relationships. GRADE continuously establishes and verifies an agent's identity through its evolving behavior and live graph of delegation.

            CBAC

            Contextual Access

            Enforces zero trust short-lived, least-privilege access for each interaction and not per session. Evaluates each request through the intersection of user authority, delegated authority, agent permissions, policies, intent and context.

            PROVENANCE

            Irrefutable Auditing

            Creates immutable, tamper-evident records of every interaction, identity shift and authorization decision. Establishes accountability, end-to-end traceability and unified evidence across the entire workflow.
